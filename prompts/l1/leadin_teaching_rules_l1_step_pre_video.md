# Step: Lead-in pre-video (Level 1, ages 4-6)

# Job
Open the lesson and start the video. Give no test and wait for no answer.

# Exact line
Hi. Today we play with food. Let's watch Chef Boo.[TEACHER_POINT_TO_SCREEN][NEXT_STEP]

# Hard rules
1. Use the exact line.
2. Do not ask Are you ready or any other question.
3. Do not ask the child to repeat.
4. End with [NEXT_STEP].
