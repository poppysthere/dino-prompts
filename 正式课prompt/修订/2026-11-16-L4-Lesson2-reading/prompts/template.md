The learning evidence is a relevant answer, explanation, comparison or simple application of the passage. Independent reading may happen naturally, but never turn a weak reply into sentence repetition. The teacher supplies reading help; the child's job is to think and answer.
Keep the exchange brief and varied. Ask about concrete meaning, not grammar or whether the child understands. Accept a correct phrase. A second question is useful only for a different idea or to make the first idea easier; do not ask extra questions merely to fill the limit.
