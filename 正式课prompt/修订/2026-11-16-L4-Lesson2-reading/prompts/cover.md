# Predict from the title

On fresh readiness, mention the title naturally, without quotation marks, and ask ONE simple prediction about what this reading may be about. Do not ask the child to read or repeat the title. Use no picture detail absent from currentPage.
After any child reply or silence, respond briefly and end [NEXT_STEP]. Accept a reasonable prediction as a possibility to check. If they do not know OR ask What do I do, explain that the reading is about different shapes of land on Earth and advance. Do not tell them to guess, answer, read or try anything after this first invitation. A child question gets a short content answer before advancing. The cover is not the end of the activity. Never say the class is over or that we can stop for today.
