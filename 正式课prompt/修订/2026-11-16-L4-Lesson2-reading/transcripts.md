## reciter

### page_0

Teacher: Today’s reading is called Landforms on Earth. What do you think it may be about? [STUDENT_TALK]

Child: Landforms on Earth

Teacher: That is the title. This reading may be about different shapes of land on Earth. [NEXT_STEP]

### page_1

Teacher: Where does a valley lie? [STUDENT_TALK]

Child: One interesting landform is a valley. A valley is a low area of land. It lies between mountains. Sometimes, there may be a river that flows through the valley.

Teacher: Yes, a valley lies between mountains. What may flow through a valley? [STUDENT_TALK]

Child: A river can flow through it.

Teacher: Right, a river may flow through a valley. A valley is a low area between mountains. [NEXT_STEP]

### page_2

Teacher: What is a glacier made of? [STUDENT_TALK]

Child: Glaciers are an important landform. However, most people will never see a glacier. Glaciers are found in colder areas. Each glacier is a huge piece of ice. It moves over the land.

Teacher: A glacier is a huge piece of ice. Where are glaciers found? [STUDENT_TALK]

Child: In cold areas.

Teacher: Yes, glaciers are found in colder areas. They are huge pieces of ice that move over the land. [NEXT_STEP]

### page_3

Teacher: What surrounds an island? [STUDENT_TALK]

Child: An island is another type of landform. An island is land surrounded by water. On some islands, there are mountains, valleys, and cliffs.

Teacher: Right, water surrounds an island. What is one landform that can be on an island? [STUDENT_TALK]

Child: Mountains, valleys and cliffs.

Teacher: Yes. An island can have mountains, valleys, and cliffs on it. [NEXT_STEP]

### page_4

Teacher: Where does the text say a cave is often found? [STUDENT_TALK]

Child: A cave is often found in a cliff or a mountain.

Teacher: Right. A cave is often found in a cliff or a mountain. [NEXT_STEP]

### page_5

Teacher: What kind of islands do people especially love? [STUDENT_TALK]

Child: Most people love islands. They especially love islands with beaches on them.

Teacher: Yes, people especially love islands with beaches on them. [TEMPLATE_FINISH]
