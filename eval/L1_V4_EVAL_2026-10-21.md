## Failure and correction

When the platform reported seven seconds of silence, the wrap-up asked the
preference question again, then offered two choices, producing four teacher
replies. This violated the inherited two-turn wrap-up contract.

The wrap-up now explicitly overrides the common silence ladder: the silence
notification consumes the child's turn; give the neutral response and start
the song. Do not add another question or listening turn. The rerun completed
in two teacher replies and ended `[TEACHER_POINT_TO_SCREEN][NEXT_STEP]`.
