# L1 V4 IPA revision evaluation — 2026-10-21

Replaces letter-and-dot sound cues with individual IPA phonemes, each inside
its own slash pair. Only the common voice-engine rule and the three vocabulary
sound-model sections changed. Lesson flow, action tags, openings, handoffs,
success criteria, and turn limits are unchanged by this revision.

| Word | Required sound model |
| --- | --- |
| apple | `/æ/ /p/ /ə/ /l/` |
| bread | `/b/ /r/ /ɛ/ /d/` |
| juice | `/dʒ/ /uː/ /s/` |

Spaces separate phonemes; no ellipses. `/dʒ/` is a single affricate. The child
is invited to say the whole word, not name letters or read IPA.
