What changed / 修改内容

  • ASCII punctuation and straight apostrophes, with explicit exceptions for approved IPA and platform tags. No written laughs or cheers.
  • A1-sized sentences, clear child instructions, child-first responses, no invented success on silence, and no figurative “Good eyes”.
  • Single syllables use phonemes; multisyllabic words use syllables. Specific sound requests take priority. Cow's /aʊ/ stays one phoneme.
  • Help uses the existing retry allowance. A question mentioning the word does not count as mastery. After the cake question, respond and close without repeating it.

Harness and evidence / 测试方式与证据

The harness composes the local common rules plus each stage template, runs scripted child turns through Forge, saves raw replies before checking, and verifies punctuation, answer-first behavior, retry limits, state progression, and final tag placement. Agent qualitative spot-checks supplement these mechanical checks; no independent human review is claimed.

Two genuine failures in the broad 107-case run were repaired: cow's diphthong was split, and a wrap-up question received “Me too”. All word and wrap-up cases were then rerun (61/61), with three additional repetitions of each failure (6/6). Sentence cases were rerun after one final wording edit (28/28). Unchanged lead-in evidence (18/18) is retained only after matching source hashes. The consolidated total is 107 distinct cases, not a claim that one untouched run passed everything.
